CROSS-PROVIDER (batch 4) — the sharpest finding
  * CONCRETE MECHANISM for the empty-snippet relevance bypass: both baidu.py (line 231) and
    bing.py (line 189) have a bare-anchor fallback that appends
    (href, title, "") — snippet is EMPTY, and when the container selector fails entirely
